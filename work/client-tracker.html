One portal where an owner sees every client's hours and bills them

A multi-tenant portal built for Sonia Associates: the owner sees time logged across clients, approves it, invoices it and exports reports.

The problem

The owner needed one place to see time across every client, approve it and bill it, while each client sees only its own work.

What I built

  • Multi-tenant owner and client portal with tenant isolation, so a client only ever sees its own data
  • Time tracking with approvals, and invites by magic link
  • CSV and PDF reports for invoicing

Key decisions

  • Decisions written down. Architecture choices are recorded as ADRs.
  • Tested in a real browser. Playwright end-to-end tests cover the main flows.

Results

  • 1view across every client
  • 2export formats: CSV and PDF
