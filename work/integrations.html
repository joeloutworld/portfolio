The problem

Webhooks get missed, retried and delivered twice. Without a check, the CRM and the loan system slowly disagree about contacts, deals and notes.

What I built

  • Two-way CRM sync for contacts, opportunities, notes and conversations between the loan system and GoHighLevel, with a nightly reconcile
  • Property Watch: PropertyRadar status-change webhooks become classified events in a Supabase ledger and then CRM actions, with a nightly backfill and a cost-aware skip-trace waterfall
  • A lead compliance pipeline: two-source owner checks, do-not-call and litigator scrubbing, and three-way routing (contactable, excluded, manual review) with the evidence kept
  • An SMS and call analytics dashboard (Next.js, PostgreSQL, Recharts, 14 migrations) fed by an hourly sync, holding no CRM credentials

Key decisions

  • Fail closed. A lead with missing or conflicting compliance data goes to manual review, never to the dialer.
  • A ledger first. Every event is stored before it acts, so a nightly job can replay or repair it.
  • Least privilege. The dashboard reads synced data and never holds CRM keys.

Results

  • 2-wayCRM sync, reconciled nightly
